# 23 — Acute Event → Leaf Impairment Trace, v0

**Status:** conceptual integration trace. This document begins with an existing **rooted, fully deployed Active Leaf** and one abstract external occurrence, **Acute Environmental Event A**. It ends with the mature Leaf's post-event manifestation state. Damage before Full Deployment belongs to developing-manifestation biology and Tuber-funded construction accounting ([30](30_EMERGENCE_INVESTMENT_AND_FULL_DEPLOYMENT_ACCOUNTING_BOUNDARY_V0.md)); possible continuation, imperfect deployment and failure are traced in [31](31_DAMAGED_EMERGENCE_TO_IMPERFECT_DEPLOYMENT_V0.md). This mature-Leaf event trace also applies after a Leaf deployed imperfectly, starting from its actual mature state ([32](32_IMPERFECTLY_DEPLOYED_MATURE_LEAF_STABILIZATION_OR_DECLINE_V0.md)), and to a Leaf already impaired by an earlier mature event. No event type, species response, scale, threshold, duration, formula or persistent biological outcome is defined.

It closes the conceptual handoff exposed by the [Biological Year Walkthrough](22_BIOLOGICAL_YEAR_WALKTHROUGH_V0.md). [Environment/Fit v0](12_ENVIRONMENT_AND_FIT_MODEL_V0.md) governs World conditions and the derived biological boundary; [condition v0](14_CURRENT_BIOLOGICAL_CONDITION_V0.md) supplies individual context; [the harm specification](18_PHASE_DAMAGE_CORE_RECOVERY_AND_BLOOM_MATURITY_V0.md) separates manifestation from persistent harm. The [biology ↔ magic boundary](20_BIOLOGY_MAGIC_BOUNDARY_AND_ASTRAL_READINESS_V0.md) constrains the inhabited case.

> The World reports an occurrence. Local context shapes exposure. The Leaf's own response determines its impairment.

## The handoff

| Step | Owner and result | What it does not decide |
|---|---|---|
| **World event** | World knows that Event A occurs at a place and time, with local extent, intensity and duration where relevant. | Whether this Leaf is injured. |
| **Experienced exposure** | World-side local context, including protection and the Leaf's position, mediates how much of that event reaches the rooted manifestation. | Biological susceptibility or damage. |
| **Biological response** | At the derived World × Amorpho boundary, the Leaf's current state and the individual's susceptibility determine what the exposure does to this manifestation. | A fixed damage rule from event intensity alone. |
| **Post-event Leaf state** | The Amorpho carries any structural and functional impairment on its current Leaf manifestation. | Active-phase productivity, retreat or persistent Tuber consequence. |

This is an **ownership chain, not an event implementation**. Environmental Fit v0 models sustained conditions and has no discrete-event field. Its provisional *exposure / protection* dimension marks a possible vulnerability input; this trace neither validates it nor forces Event A into the existing five-dimension Local Environment State. How acute events are represented and evaluated remains AMO-Q020 and AMO-Q069. Fit remains a derived evaluation, never a store of weather, events or Leaf health (AMO-D035–AMO-D037).

## Event is not exposure

An event may cover a broad area while a sheltered Leaf experiences little of it. A more modest event at a highly exposed position may reach the Leaf strongly. Local shelter, a building, a greenhouse or a future protective arrangement can modify exposure without changing the fact that the event occurred. None is designed here. The World supplies local environmental facts; it never emits a verdict such as “Leaf function lost.”

Experienced exposure is still **not** damage. A's biological response may depend on current condition, existing manifestation impairment, developmental context, prior stress and future approved species or individual traits. Those belong to the Amorpho side, not the World. The event-to-response evaluation compares World-side exposure with that biological context; its representation and susceptibility rules remain open (AMO-Q020, AMO-Q086). No one-to-one severity mapping is implied.

## Smallest useful Leaf output

The post-event record needs two distinct descriptions of the **current Leaf**:

1. **Structural integrity** — what physical Leaf structure remains and in what condition. This is phase-specific; it ends with the manifestation (AMO-D074).
2. **Leaf Functional Capacity** — how much useful biological work this Leaf can still potentially perform. It is not a percentage read from structural appearance (AMO-D091, L51).

They are not interchangeable. A conspicuously damaged Leaf can retain substantial useful function; a modest-looking impairment can sharply reduce function. Neither state is Tuber vitality, reserves, stress load or Developmental Maturity. This trace does not prescribe stored fields, scalar meters or a structural-to-functional function (AMO-Q086, AMO-Q116).

Two **assessments**, rather than additional required state variables, accompany the record:

- **Same-Leaf recovery prospect:** whether this current Leaf might stabilize or regain function while it remains active. For a fully deployed Leaf, this is separate from the persistent individual's possible **replacement emergence**; neither prospect guarantees an outcome. The distinction is traced in [24_SAME_PHASE_LEAF_RECOVERY_V0.md](24_SAME_PHASE_LEAF_RECOVERY_V0.md) (AMO-D094, AMO-Q103, AMO-Q084). Recording “undetermined” is more accurate than assuming that good later Fit repairs the Leaf.
- **Immediate phase viability:** whether this Leaf still exists and can continue as an active manifestation immediately after the event. This observes the present state; it does not choose a Senescence transition. The eventual retreat rule belongs to AMO-Q087 and AMO-Q101.

Water handling, mechanical stability and other possible component functions might later explain the capacity assessment. None becomes a separate v0 variable merely because it can be named. Likewise, recovery of **Leaf function** is not recovery of persistent Tuber state (AMO-D074).

## Worked trace: one event, sheltered Leaf

**Before.** Abstract Individual A is rooted in Active Leaf. The Leaf is physically intact and doing useful biological work. Its individual condition is sound. No real species or biological response value is assumed.

**Occurrence.** The World records **Acute Environmental Event A** at A's location. That fact contains no Leaf damage instruction. A local shelter changes how strongly the rooted Leaf is exposed. The shelter does not erase the event, and this trace assigns it no protective rating.

**Response.** Some exposure reaches the Leaf. Interpreted against A's current biological and manifestation state, it visibly disrupts part of the structure. The disruption reduces Leaf Functional Capacity, but useful function remains. This is a possible qualitative response, not a calibrated rule for Event A.

**Recorded result.** The current Leaf is **visibly impaired but structurally present**, with **reduced yet substantial Functional Capacity**. Same-phase structural or functional repair is **undetermined** (AMO-Q103). The Leaf **can continue immediately**; whether a later phase transition occurs is not decided here. **Stop.** Nothing in this record says how much biological work the Leaf will later perform or what the persistent Tuber will gain or lose.

## Contrast: same event, different exposure and susceptibility

Hold **Event A** fixed. In a counterfactual, A occupies a more exposed local position and enters with a less favourable current condition and some prior Leaf impairment. The World event is unchanged, but experienced exposure and biological susceptibility differ. The Leaf could end structurally present with a **much poorer Functional Capacity**, or with more obvious structural disruption. Its immediate continuation and same-phase recovery prospects require their own assessment; neither can be copied from the sheltered trace.

The counterfactual establishes a boundary, not a prediction of magnitude: **World event severity does not determine Leaf impairment by itself**. Local protection can change exposure; the Amorpho's state can change response. Conversely, similar structural appearance need not imply similar biological function (AMO-D091, L51).

## Rooted and inhabited contexts

The worked biological trace is rooted. If A is **astrally inhabited**, movement, shelter-seeking, defensive action, protection and equipment may alter what the animated form experiences. Those are extension points for exposure and magical gameplay, not mechanics defined here. Under the existing rule, **normal biological progression is suspended during embodiment** (AMO-D084, L49) — but suspension is not invulnerability. The animated form is the biological manifestation, so an effect that actually **reaches the body** is real biological Leaf damage and enters this same impairment pathway, while an effect stopped short of it has combat or protection consequences only ([35](35_COMBAT_PROTECTION_MANIFESTATION_BODY_DAMAGE_AND_BIOLOGICAL_PERSISTENCE_V0.md), AMO-D120, AMO-D122, AMO-D123, L58). There is no separate combat-specific biological damage model, and no proportional translation from combat state to Leaf condition (AMO-D121). What determines whether an effect reaches the body, and the magnitude that follows, remain AMO-Q026, AMO-Q086 and AMO-Q109. On astral exit the biological Leaf state — including any damage taken while embodied — continues; exit itself repairs nothing (AMO-D072).

## Where the trace stops

The output is **post-event Active Leaf state**, with structural integrity and Functional Capacity kept separate, plus the two limited assessments above. It is an input to later active-phase work, **not** a verdict about that work. AMO-Q116 owns how capacity, Environmental Fit and Remaining Productive Opportunity produce biological productivity. AMO-Q087 and AMO-Q101 own retreat. AMO-Q108 and AMO-Q118 own the boundary to actual persistent Tuber loss. This document evaluates none of those outcomes and makes no reserve, maturity or Tuber accounting claim.

**AMO-Q020** still owns which acute events exist and how a discrete World occurrence becomes local exposure; **AMO-Q086** still owns phase-specific integrity and the Leaf's response, including the structural-to-functional mapping. [The next trace](24_SAME_PHASE_LEAF_RECOVERY_V0.md) follows this output over the same active period: **AMO-Q103** owns recovery of the current Leaf, and **AMO-Q084** owns possible replacement emergence. A post-event state may lead either to continued mature recovery or, if the event destroyed the Leaf's central biological role, straight to Functional Collapse ([33](33_MATURE_LEAF_FUNCTIONAL_COLLAPSE_BOUNDARY_V0.md), AMO-D114). An event never routes directly to Replacement: that boundary is crossed first. Their exact representations and rules remain open. No new decision, law or question was needed for the acute-event handoff.
