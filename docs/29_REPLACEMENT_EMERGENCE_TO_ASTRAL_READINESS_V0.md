# 29 — Replacement Emergence to Astral Readiness, v0

**Status:** conceptual biology ↔ magic handoff using owner-supplied biological direction. Replacement Leaf is the tested route; the underlying **Manifestation Emergence** process also applies to primary Leaf and Bloom. This defines no species facts, durations, costs, formulas, interface, combat mapping or implementation.

> **The Tuber does not spawn playable forms. It grows manifestations.**
>
> **A manifestation must finish becoming itself before the Warden can normally become it.**

## 1. Persistent individual and biological construction

The Tuber remains the one persistent individual throughout Emergence. Identity, genetics, lineage, Developmental Maturity, condition, reserves, previous expenditure, damage and biological history carry forward. A Leaf or Bloom is a new temporary manifestation of that same individual, never a new individual. A Replacement Leaf is not repair of the old Leaf, a respawn or resurrection: the Tuber survived, chose or was compelled to attempt salvage, and now must build the new structure (AMO-D094–AMO-D096).

**Emergence is biological construction, not instant manifestation.** Committing to a route begins an investment; it does not deliver a body. The Tuber supports the protective path, developing structure and deployment over biological time. Excellent Environmental Fit can support that process but cannot make it instantaneous. During unembodied Emergence, the World, environment and biological simulation continue, leaving the developing manifestation vulnerable. The existing suspension of biology during playable embodiment remains authoritative if an old manifestation can still be inhabited; whether such overlap occurs is open (AMO-D084, AMO-Q084). Warden input alone cannot fast-forward Emergence. Human care may alter real conditions; biology responds to those conditions (AMO-D035–AMO-D037, L52).

## 2. Shared Manifestation Emergence

**Emergence Sheath** is a conservative game-system term for the owner-supplied protective sheath-like structure or path produced from the Tuber toward the surface. It protects the developing manifestation as it advances. It is biological infrastructure, never a playable Leaf, Bloom, fighter or Tuber substitute. Its exact botanical name and species interpretation await approved external input; none is inferred here (AMO-D024).

The existing `Emergence` life-cycle state can hold the following **conceptual stages**, without making them new states or prescribing their durations:

| Stage | Biological meaning | Normal manifestation inhabitation |
|---|---|---|
| Commitment | The Tuber commits biological capacity to a Leaf or Bloom attempt. | None; no new playable body exists. |
| Sheath / path formation | The protective emergence path develops toward the surface. | None; the developing manifestation remains protected and incomplete. |
| Surface emergence | Leaf or Bloom becomes externally visible as it emerges. | None; visibility is not completion. |
| Expansion / deployment | Leaf unfolds or Bloom opens and develops its manifestation-specific architecture. | None while deployment remains incomplete. |
| **Full Deployment** | This manifestation has completed its primary emergence architecture. | Biological prerequisite met; ordinary astral gates must still be satisfied. |

After the shared foundation, Leaf and Bloom follow their own biology. A fully deployed Leaf and a fully deployed Bloom have different biological roles, costs, duration and mature damage domains. Shared emergence does not make them interchangeable. Bloom's normal Programmed Tuber Draw remains normal expenditure, not Pathological Tuber Impact merely because it spends capacity (AMO-D087, AMO-D092). No species-specific Bloom morphology is specified.

```text
PERSISTENT TUBER
  → manifestation commitment
  → Emergence Sheath / protected path
  → developing Leaf or Bloom
  → surface emergence (visible, not inhabitable)
  → manifestation-specific expansion / deployment (not inhabitable)
  → Full Leaf Deployment or Full Bloom Deployment
  → ordinary Astral Readiness and access pathway
  → potentially inhabitable Leaf or Bloom
```

Primary Leaf, Replacement Leaf and Bloom differ in entry context and biological purpose; they share this high-level construction process and the existing `Emergence` state (AMO-D070, AMO-D096). No instant-spawn exception follows from Warden authorship or a powerful Tuber.

## 3. Full Deployment and the magic handoff

**Full Deployment** means the current manifestation has completed enough of its primary emergence architecture to enter mature state. For a Leaf, its main unfolding and expansion are complete. For a Bloom, its emergence and opening have reached the mature manifested form. It is complete **for this manifestation**, not necessarily as large as another Leaf or Bloom, in perfect condition, or at a maximum species scale. A smaller Replacement Leaf can be fully deployed. A damaged attempt may likewise deploy imperfectly; deployment carries the actual constructed limitations forward rather than healing them ([31](31_DAMAGED_EMERGENCE_TO_IMPERFECT_DEPLOYMENT_V0.md)).

Before Full Deployment, a visible or externally exposed developing Leaf or Bloom is **not normally inhabitable**: no walking, fighting or exploring as it. At or after Full Deployment, normal inhabitation **may become possible**, subject to the existing physical Anchor, life-cycle access, biological suitability and Astral Readiness gates (AMO-D063, AMO-D086). Full Deployment does not itself guarantee instant Astral Readiness or entry. The exact readiness trigger, Signal change and access boundary remain AMO-Q085, AMO-Q112 and AMO-Q115.

The stage boundary also assigns damage to the right model. Before Full Deployment, damage belongs to developing-manifestation biology and may lead to compensated continuation, imperfect deployment or failure; its mechanisms remain open (AMO-Q086). After Full Leaf Deployment, mature Leaf impairment and same-phase recovery apply ([23](23_ACUTE_EVENT_TO_LEAF_IMPAIRMENT_V0.md), [24](24_SAME_PHASE_LEAF_RECOVERY_V0.md)). After Full Bloom Deployment, mature Bloom damage belongs to its own existing/future rules. Deployment does not heal developmental damage or prior Tuber costs, or erase a failed primary Leaf's history. Astral Readiness remains separate even for a fully deployed compromised form.

The [Emergence accounting boundary](30_EMERGENCE_INVESTMENT_AND_FULL_DEPLOYMENT_ACCOUNTING_BOUNDARY_V0.md) makes this an ownership handoff as well: Tuber-funded construction investment accumulates until Full Deployment; from there the mature manifestation owns its immediate performance and damage state (AMO-D109–AMO-D111, L55). Earlier investment remains spent. Full Deployment still does not itself confer Astral Readiness.

Strategic Warden presence during the preceding Astral Window does not turn into automatic embodiment of an emerging structure. Whether that direct presence persists or ends, and what awareness remains during Emergence, are open (AMO-Q085, AMO-Q119). Emergence requires no rule that traps the Warden for the entire construction period. The Astral Window is the earlier Tuber routing opportunity; Emergence is the subsequent biological attempt. The exact reachability handoff between them remains open.

## 4. Replacement context, cost and failure

After Leaf collapse, biology may autonomously select Replacement or a Warden may select it while feasible and directly present ([28](28_ASTRAL_WINDOW_END_TO_END_TRACE_V0.md), AMO-D098–AMO-D101). Either selection enters the same biological Emergence process. Commitment diverts the Tuber from the immediate retreat path into another active manifestation attempt; it does not make later Dormancy impossible.

Replacement uses remaining Tuber capacity, condition and opportunity after the primary Leaf's cost and loss. It may produce a reduced Leaf; no ratio or combat result follows (AMO-Q084, AMO-Q110). The whole construction is a biological investment, not a fee paid only at choice. The timing and distribution of that investment remain open. A compelled route follows the same biological process, time and vulnerability as an autonomous one (L52).

Eligibility to **attempt** Replacement does not guarantee Full Deployment. Changing conditions, damage or insufficiency may prevent completion. If the attempt fails, no playable Leaf appears; the persistent Tuber remains, carrying expenditure and history, and the life cycle may route through retreat and consolidation toward Dormancy. Investment accumulates with developmental commitment, so late failure generally loses more already-funded construction than early failure; visible damage severity is a separate assessment ([30](30_EMERGENCE_INVESTMENT_AND_FULL_DEPLOYMENT_ACCOUNTING_BOUNDARY_V0.md)). Neither an automatic retry nor a trapped Emergence state follows. Exact failure criteria and subsequent routing remain AMO-Q084 and AMO-Q101. Spent capacity, including after failure, is not by itself Pathological Tuber Impact; actual persistent harm uses the existing boundary (AMO-D092, AMO-Q118).

The same high-level possibility exists for Bloom emergence: commitment can fail before Full Bloom Deployment without creating a playable Bloom. The later lifecycle response remains open; no new Bloom-specific failure mechanism is implied.

## 5. Worked traces

**A — autonomous Replacement to possible inhabitation.** The primary Leaf collapses. Biology selects feasible Replacement without Warden presence. The Tuber commits capacity, forms an Emergence Sheath, sends a new Leaf through it and supports expansion. The Leaf is visible before it is inhabitable. Full Leaf Deployment creates a mature manifestation; ordinary Signal, access and Astral Readiness rules may later permit embodiment. No Warden shortcut occurred.

**B — Warden-compelled Replacement.** The plant preferred retreat, but direct Warden presence selected feasible Replacement in the Astral Window. The Warden receives no instant fighter. The Tuber follows the same sheath, emergence and deployment process at full biological cost. Only a fully deployed Leaf can enter the normal Readiness pathway. Route authorship does not change emergence physics (AMO-Q120).

**C — visible but incomplete.** A developing Leaf has reached the surface and is expanding. It can be seen, but normal embodiment is unavailable. Full Deployment is the next biological prerequisite; the remaining astral gates still apply afterwards. The same rule holds for a visible, opening Bloom.

**D — reduced but complete.** A Tuber with less remaining capacity produces a smaller Replacement Leaf. It finishes deploying its own architecture. It is fully deployed despite its reduced scale; suitability and Readiness remain separate questions.

**E — failed Replacement.** The Tuber commits and emergence begins, then conditions worsen enough that this attempt cannot complete. No fully deployed or playable Leaf results. The same individual retains the cost and routes toward retreat, consolidation and Dormancy if biology permits. Failure is not automatically Pathological Tuber Impact.

**F — Bloom emergence.** A biologically eligible Tuber commits to Bloom, forms the shared protective path and develops the Bloom. Surface visibility and opening do not permit normal embodiment. Full Bloom Deployment precedes any normal Readiness and inhabitation. The mature Bloom then follows its distinct reproductive biology and Programmed Tuber Draw.

## 6. Boundaries and acceptance

AMO-Q084 keeps emergence duration, optional sub-phases, manifestation-specific variation, old/new Leaf overlap and the full-deployment criterion. AMO-Q086 keeps the mechanisms of developmental damage, compensation and mature-state derivation; the conceptual outcome range is traced in [31](31_DAMAGED_EMERGENCE_TO_IMPERFECT_DEPLOYMENT_V0.md). AMO-Q101 keeps cost distribution, success/failure conditions and post-failure routing; AMO-Q110 keeps reduced scale. AMO-Q085/AMO-Q115 keep access and Signal during the transition; AMO-Q112 keeps the post-deployment Readiness trigger; AMO-Q119 keeps Warden connection continuity. No new question is required.

| Check | Result |
|---|---|
| Normal Leaf and Bloom emergence | Both require sheath/path, development and Full Deployment before potential inhabitation. |
| Visible ≠ inhabitable | An exposed Leaf or Bloom remains non-playable while deploying. |
| Replacement ≠ respawn | Route selection begins biological construction; no instant fighter appears. |
| Reduced ≠ incomplete | A smaller Replacement can still be fully deployed. |
| Failed attempt | No playable manifestation forms; retreat remains possible and the Tuber persists. |
| Tuber non-playability | The Tuber and Emergence Sheath are never locomotion or combat bodies. |
| Cost and time | Autonomous and compelled routes use the same ongoing biological process; Warden input does not fast-forward it. |
| Mature damage boundary | Development damage precedes Full Deployment; mature manifestation rules follow it. |
| Persistent history | Full Deployment does not reset identity, Tuber condition, cost or prior Leaf failure. |

**Result:** biological construction precedes magical playability. Magic can choose the route; biology still has to build the body.
