# 28 — Astral Window End-to-End Trace, v0

**Status:** integration trace of accepted rules, not a new routing system. One abstract individual is followed from Leaf collapse through the available routes. No species facts, durations, thresholds, probabilities, interface or implementation are assigned.

**Acceptance question:** Can the individual enter, pass through and leave an Astral Window coherently with or without Warden intervention, while preserving autonomous biology, One Consciousness, biological cost and Deep Dormancy?

## 1. Starting collapse and persistent individual

One anchored individual has a viable Tuber and an active Leaf. A catastrophic event leaves the Leaf unable to perform its central biological role, after feasible same-Leaf stabilization has been considered ([25](25_TUBER_FUNDED_MANIFESTATION_AND_REPLACEMENT_ROUTING_V0.md)). The current Leaf ceases to be a valid active manifestation. **The Tuber does not disappear:** the same individual remains alive with its identity, condition, Developmental Maturity, reserves, capacity and biological history. Leaf collapse is neither Tuber death nor a reset.

The collapse brings this individual to the functional-collapse routing point in the existing [life-cycle state machine](17_LIFE_CYCLE_STATE_MACHINE_V0.md). It does not add a state. This trace takes the **acute** path, where commitment and the end of the manifestation effectively coincide; a gradual course passes through a terminal period while the Leaf still physically exists before the Window applies ([33](33_MATURE_LEAF_FUNCTIONAL_COLLAPSE_BOUNDARY_V0.md), AMO-D115).

## 2. Transitional Tuber and the Astral Window

The Tuber does not jump straight to Deep Dormancy. After the Leaf loses its active role, the individual can remain biologically active and astrally reachable during a transitional period. **That reachable period is the Astral Window** (AMO-D099). Its exact opening and duration remain AMO-Q085; it is defined by biological state, not by a clock or a display.

An Astral Signal can make the anchored individual detectable and potentially represent it as reachable to the Warden through the existing astral system. Detection says that access may remain; it does not convey control, exact remaining time, exact capacity, route eligibility or a forecast (AMO-Q092, AMO-Q115, AMO-Q119). Physical Anchor and phase access still matter (AMO-D063, AMO-D101).

**Reachability is opportunity, not playability.** Direct presence here is strategic contact with the persistent individual. It creates no Leaf, restores no old Leaf and animates no Tuber. The Warden cannot move, fight or explore as the Tuber. Playable embodiment requires a suitable manifestation later and the ordinary gates, including Astral Readiness ([20](20_BIOLOGY_MAGIC_BOUNDARY_AND_ASTRAL_READINESS_V0.md), AMO-D086).

The Window is not a `WAITING_FOR_PLAYER` state. Autonomous decisions and route execution may begin while the individual remains reachable. A Warden arriving then can influence only a **still-available** biological routing opportunity; presence cannot undo a route already taken. The precise reachability handoff after Replacement Emergence begins remains open (AMO-Q085, AMO-Q115).

## 3. Routing order

At the routing point, biology first determines feasible routes. For the main traces, both **Replacement Emergence** and **early retreat toward Dormancy** are feasible. The individual's own biology then prefers the route serving its continuation from its current state and environment (AMO-D098). If no Warden establishes direct presence while that choice remains available, the autonomous route executes. If one does, the Warden may select another feasible route (AMO-D100–AMO-D101). Biology executes either selection and pays its full cost (L52).

No eligibility formula or autonomous selection policy is inferred here; those remain AMO-Q101. Route authorship is conceptually distinguishable, but whether it is stored remains AMO-Q120.

## 4. Trace A — autonomous Replacement

1. The Leaf suffers functional collapse. The viable Tuber and its persistent state remain.
2. The Astral Window opens during transitional reachability. The individual may be detected as reachable, but the Tuber is not playable.
3. The Warden is elsewhere and establishes no direct presence. Biology continues without waiting.
4. Biology finds Replacement and retreat feasible. Given a capable Tuber, favorable conditions and substantial remaining opportunity, the plant's own preference is Replacement.
5. The plant autonomously commits to Replacement. The Tuber begins its normal biological investment; elapsed opportunity is not restored.
6. Replacement Emergence begins in the existing intra-cycle loop. Biological time passes and a new, generally reduced Leaf develops from the same individual.
7. The new Leaf remains non-inhabitable through Emergence Sheath formation, surface emergence and deployment. **Full Deployment** precedes its normal Astral Readiness pathway ([29](29_REPLACEMENT_EMERGENCE_TO_ASTRAL_READINESS_V0.md)); Signal, access and the exact Readiness trigger remain open (AMO-Q084, AMO-Q085, AMO-Q112, AMO-Q115). No retroactive Warden choice occurs.

The individual's biological route is autonomous even though the Warden could have reached it earlier.

## 5. Trace B — Warden overrides Retreat

Use the same viable-Tuber and collapsed-Leaf setup, with conditions that make conservation preferable to the plant while Replacement remains feasible.

1. The Leaf collapses; the persistent Tuber enters the reachable transition and the Astral Window is open.
2. Biology finds Replacement and retreat feasible. Its autonomous preference is retreat toward Dormancy.
3. Before that choice is executed, the Warden notices reachability and establishes **direct strategic astral presence** through the existing access path. Radar awareness or an Anchor relationship alone would not select a route.
4. The Warden's one direct presence is committed here. The Warden cannot simultaneously directly act through the Human body, another Amorpho or another Tuber routing point (AMO-D028, AMO-D101).
5. The Warden selects feasible Replacement instead of the plant's preferred retreat. Biology executes that route at its full normal Tuber expenditure and over biological time. Magic changes route selection; it does not construct a Leaf or discount the cost.
6. Replacement Emergence begins; a new Leaf develops through the shared biological process ([29](29_REPLACEMENT_EMERGENCE_TO_ASTRAL_READINESS_V0.md)). During emergence the Warden cannot move, fight or explore as the Tuber **or the incomplete Leaf**. Full Deployment comes before any normal access and Astral Readiness pathway.

This is Warden-influenced route authorship in concept, without a history-storage rule (AMO-Q120).

## 6. Trace C — Warden accepts biology

Replacement and retreat are feasible, and the plant prefers retreat. The Warden establishes direct presence while the routing opportunity remains, then selects retreat too. Biology carries out the same conservation route. Presence did not require disagreement; the Warden consciously accepts the plant-preferred feasible route, with no biological discount or new body. Whether agreement is recorded as Warden authorship remains AMO-Q120.

## 7. Trace D — impossible override

The Leaf collapses and the Astral Window remains open, but the Tuber is too compromised to fund Replacement. The Warden establishes direct presence. Biology's feasible set contains retreat, **not Replacement**. The Warden cannot select Replacement, replenish capacity by presence or make an impossible Leaf. Retreat proceeds through the existing lifecycle toward Dormancy. Astral leverage cannot override biological impossibility (AMO-D100, L52).

## 8. Trace E — missed Window

1. The Leaf collapses; the viable Tuber enters a reachable transition and the Astral Window opens.
2. The Warden is occupied elsewhere. Autonomous biology finds feasible routes, prefers retreat and begins it without waiting for the Warden.
3. The individual passes through the existing Senescence and Early Dormancy path toward Tuber consolidation, then enters Deep Dormancy.
4. Deep Dormancy brings Astral Silence and closes this Window. The Warden arrives later; strategic override is unavailable, and the historical route cannot be rewritten (AMO-D060, AMO-D088, AMO-D099).

The individual remains persistent and alive in Deep Dormancy. Future-cycle access, if any, belongs to later biological transitions, not this missed routing decision.

## 9. Presence does not stop biological time

**The Astral Window is not a pause. Biology keeps moving.** Direct Tuber presence is not playable embodiment, so embodiment's suspension of normal biology does not apply (AMO-D084, AMO-D099). The environment continues to act; condition and feasibility may change; the plant may begin an autonomous route; Deep Dormancy may arrive. A connection never freezes the individual until the Warden decides. If a route is no longer feasible or the routing opportunity has passed, presence cannot recover that choice.

Astral Capacity can sustain broader Anchor relationships, but it is not a second direct presence or a remote routing command (AMO-D067, AMO-D101). **One consciousness. One direct intervention at a time.**

## 10. Handoff and unresolved boundaries

Replacement uses the existing `Active Leaf → Emergence (replacement) → Active Leaf` loop. The Tuber biologically builds the new Leaf at full cost; **Full Deployment** is required before normal Leaf inhabitation may become possible ([29](29_REPLACEMENT_EMERGENCE_TO_ASTRAL_READINESS_V0.md)). Signal, access and Readiness during the handoff remain with AMO-Q084, AMO-Q085, AMO-Q112 and AMO-Q115. This trace does not assert exactly when the transitional Tuber-access situation changes after emergence begins.

Retreat uses existing Senescence and Early Dormancy toward Deep Dormancy. **Deep Dormancy closes the door** with Astral Silence and no override. The exact access boundary within the preceding transition remains AMO-Q085; no numeric duration is needed.

AMO-Q101 still owns functional-collapse and route-eligibility criteria and the autonomous policy. AMO-Q119 still owns how direct presence starts and ends, its player-facing knowledge and related access mechanics. AMO-Q120 owns whether route authorship is recorded. Radar detail, warnings and exact Signal behavior remain open in their existing questions. No new question, decision or law is needed.

## 11. Acceptance checks

**Result: yes.** The individual can enter and pass through the Astral Window on autonomous and Warden-influenced routes. Retreat exits at Deep Dormancy; Replacement hands off to Emergence without assuming an exact access boundary. The trace exposes no missing invariant. Biology determines feasible paths, autonomous biology acts unless direct Warden presence intervenes, and magic creates leverage while biology keeps the bill.
