# 50 — Warden Biological Information and Roster Judgement Model, v0

**Status:** qualitative information-architecture pass using owner-supplied design direction, including two addenda on the **Astral Anchor** as an information channel and on **Warden–Amorpho Astral Attunement**. It resolves the qualitative core of AMO-Q044 for biological judgement. It designs no HUD, icon, journal, scanner, diagnostic, forecast, alert, sensation, percentage, countdown, probability model, assistant, progression system or species fact, and it changes no biological rule.

> **Know enough to judge, not enough to calculate.**

## 1. Purpose

Docs [48](48_ROSTER_TIMING_AND_ONE_CONSCIOUSNESS_BIOLOGICAL_OPPORTUNITY_TRACE_V0.md) and [49](49_COMBAT_PILLARS_AND_BIOLOGICAL_ROSTER_STRATEGY_V0.md) traced roster choice with design-level omniscience. Play cannot. This pass answers:

> What can the Warden reasonably know, infer or remain uncertain about when deciding which Amorpho to inhabit?

The direction is that **uncertainty should create judgement, not randomness**: no perfect biological readout by default, and enough evidence to make decisions that mean something.

## 2. Biological truth and Warden knowledge

| | |
|---|---|
| **Biological Truth** | what is actually so: lifecycle position, Tuber condition, Remaining Productive Opportunity, Recovery Ceiling, Core Viability, Intrinsic Reactivation Readiness, persistent harm, manifestation condition |
| **Warden Knowledge** | what this Warden can currently observe, remember, infer, estimate — and remain uncertain about |

They are not identical, and **biological truth exists independently of what the Warden knows about it** (AMO-D164). This extends L14 — *knowing and showing are separate* — from interface to judgement, and AMO-D051's principle that the World states conditions while the Warden decides.

**The Warden is not omniscient.** By default there is no exact access to remaining opportunity, commitment proximity, reactivation timing, Recovery Ceiling, Core Viability, reserves, pathology or future return. No internal telemetry exists.

**Uncertainty is evidence-based, not a coin flip.** An experienced Warden judges better because they know the individual, its cycles, its species tendencies, its body, its environment and what happened to it.

## 3. Knowledge sources

No source supplies complete truth; together they support judgement.

1. **Direct observation** of the manifestation and the World — the body, its form, its damage, its location, its surroundings.
2. **Individual history** — when it last emerged, how long it was rooted or embodied, damage and recovery, strong or weak prior seasons, early or late past dormancy, survived destructions and rescues, Bloom history. No event log is designed; history is a knowledge source, and future records may supplement memory so knowledge does not rest on player recall alone.
3. **General biological and species knowledge** — lifecycle strategy, dormancy tendency, extended-growth potential, flowering capability, broad environmental preferences, as far as approved input supplies them (AMO-D024).
4. **Environmental context** — where the individual is, current conditions, whether a greenhouse exists, known climate tendencies, recent weather.
5. **Astral Anchor feedback** — qualitative biological and astral sensing through the bound connection (§10).
6. **Astral Attunement** — this Warden's relationship-specific ability to interpret that feedback for this individual (§11).
7. **Warden judgement** — inference across all of the above.

**Learning stays in-world.** A Warden may learn from botanical knowledge, cultivation experience, in-world information or other players, but the game operates on approved internal truth: the Warden never queries an external database at runtime, and no research system becomes a dependency (AMO-D002, AMO-D017, AMO-D018).

**Investigation may later reduce uncertainty.** Room is left for future examination, care or diagnostic observation; no scanning or pathology system is designed.

## 4. Species tendency and individual history

> **Species tells the Warden what to expect; the individual tells its own story over time.**

Species knowledge improves priors and never reveals exact individual state. Learning a species never makes all its individuals predictable, because individual history decides (AMO-D147, AMO-D158). **Familiarity improves contextual knowledge because more history is known** — no experience stat or relationship level is involved.

## 5. What is observable, inferable and uncertain

An information architecture, not a set of interface categories:

| **Directly observable or known** | **Strongly inferable** | **Fundamentally uncertain without further evidence** |
|---|---|---|
| manifestation type, Leaf or Bloom | broad lifecycle position | exact Core Viability |
| visible body condition: scars, missing architecture, fresh or compensated damage, terminal yellowing | whether current opportunity is likely valuable | exact Recovery Ceiling |
| current location — fully knowable | whether recovery is progressing | exact reactivation timing |
| visible protection condition | broad Core-security expectation from known history | exact remaining opportunity |
| rooted or embodied, now | broad dormancy expectation | exact hidden persistent harm |
| known personal history and care | | |

**Body condition is among the most directly observable biological facts**, because the rooted and embodied body are one manifestation (AMO-D134): no hidden conversion is needed to know a Leaf is damaged, though its functional consequence still needs judgement. **Internal Tuber condition is less observable** — during embodiment the core sits inside the manifestation (AMO-D129) — so Core security naturally carries more uncertainty than Leaf damage. **Location is fully knowable**, so the relocation consequence of embodiment is never hidden (AMO-D161). *Appearance reveals the manifestation, not the entire persistent Core* (L51).

## 6. Lifecycle judgement

The Warden should be able to form judgements such as *actively productive*, *comfortably within the active phase*, *approaching dormancy*, *committed*, *senescing*, *deeply dormant*, *likely nearing reactivation* or *emerging* — as inferences, not player-facing labels.

**Commitment boundaries are biological truth, not interface flags** ([46](46_DORMANCY_COMMITMENT_BOUNDARY_V0.md), [33](33_MATURE_LEAF_FUNCTIONAL_COLLAPSE_BOUNDARY_V0.md)). The Warden may not know the exact instant of Dormancy or Collapse Commitment, and may infer it strongly once its consequences show. **Terminal yellowing is a deliberately strong cue** that a gradually collapsing Leaf is irreversibly terminal (AMO-D115) — more legible, by design, than most hidden core properties.

## 7. Opportunity, recovery and core security

**Remaining Productive Opportunity is judged from its components**, never read as one value ([45](45_REMAINING_PRODUCTIVE_OPPORTUNITY_QUALITATIVE_COMPOSITION_V0.md)):

- **External opportunity** — location, season and weather, controlled environment, known reachable alternatives. Often **less hidden** than internal biology.
- **Biological Growth Availability** — lifecycle position, prior behaviour, species tendency, approach and commitment cues.
- **Manifestation usability** — visible condition, known damage, recovery, cues of function.

**Recovery is inferable only imperfectly.** The Warden sees what architecture remains, whether it has stabilized, how function has returned and whether deterioration continues, and from that judges how much more this Leaf can plausibly recover ([40](40_ROOTED_MATURE_LEAF_RECOVERY_CEILING_AND_COMPENSATORY_REMODELING_V0.md)). **Demonstrated recovery is evidence**: a scarred Leaf that has already shown strong recovered function tells more than a freshly injured one.

**Core security stays substantially uncertain**, even to the owner. *If this manifestation were destroyed now, would the exposed Tuber survive?* has no guaranteed answer (AMO-D131). The Warden reasons causally:

- **inhabited almost immediately after Full Deployment, little rooted return** → the persistent individual is probably at unusual risk;
- **long, productive, well-conditioned rooted history** → Core security is probably better;
- **damage history is separate** — known direct Tuber injury, prior pathology or past depletion can undercut an otherwise strong productive history (AMO-D148–AMO-D151).

None of these is certainty. **Bloom** gives stronger evidence than Leaf appearance — substantial prior persistent capability — and still no readout of present salvageability; its owner may know more from prior Tuber history, Bloom timing, stress and Programmed Draw context (AMO-D142, AMO-D144, L63).

## 8. Dormancy and reactivation

The Warden may know species strategy, this individual's prior dormancy patterns, current environment and persistent state, and may form expectations — *probably still deeply dormant*, *perhaps nearing readiness*, *conditions now favourable* — with **no exact wake-up date** ([47](47_DEEP_DORMANCY_DURATION_AND_ACTIVE_PHASE_REACTIVATION_BOUNDARY_V0.md)). Expectation is never authority: the Tuber stays autonomous, and no prediction becomes biological command.

**Expression creates certainty.** Once Pre-Emergence and Emergence are physically and astrally evident, the Warden knows much more. Information becomes clearer as biology expresses itself, with no hidden-state reveal beforehand.

## 9. Fairness: uncertainty must stay causal

Two requirements, held together (AMO-D165):

- **Reasonable inference may be wrong.** A Warden may judge a core robust when hidden prior persistent harm made it less secure. That is allowed — uncertainty must stay real — provided the outcome follows from actual biological state and history.
- **No arbitrary reversal.** *All evidence says healthy core → inexplicably dead Tuber, with no biological causal history* is not permitted. Surprises must be grounded in real state.

> **Important decisions should have interpretable evidence.** The Warden may be uncertain beforehand, and should be able to understand afterwards why the outcome was plausible from the biology.

## 10. The Astral Anchor as an information channel

The Anchor's established ontology is unchanged: a physical, reusable object associated with the persistent individual and travelling with its core across manifestations, required for the Warden–Amorpho relationship, and **a bridge, not a tracker** (AMO-D061, AMO-D088, AMO-D132). This pass adds one role (AMO-D166):

> **The Astral Anchor may mediate qualitative biological and astral feedback from the bound individual to its Warden.** It connects awareness as well as access.

**Qualitative sensing, not telemetry.** It never provides exact reserves, Core Viability, percentages, countdowns, Recovery Ceiling, opportunity, dormancy timing, health values or location precision beyond what the Astral Signal already allows (AMO-Q115). As design space only, the kinds of thing it might convey include general vitality, instability or serious distress, the stability of the connection, whether Astral Readiness is strong, weak or absent, major transitions, severe persistent impairment and broad change in internal condition. No signal is final.

**Three channels, complementary.** *Physical observation* — what can be seen of body and World. *Historical and learned knowledge* — prior cycles, species tendencies, care. *Anchor feedback* — what the connection conveys about what is not visible. Even together they leave **Biological Truth richer than Warden Knowledge**: the Anchor may convey that the core feels severely depleted or unstable without answering whether it would survive destruction now.

**It rides on the Astral Signal and respects its boundaries.** Feedback is available only where the existing signal is: it fades through Senescence, is absent in **Deep Dormancy — Astral Silence** — and returns in Pre-Emergence (AMO-D088). It is not a channel around silence; any minimal dormant awareness would need explicit future design.

**Major transitions may be especially perceptible** — loss of Astral Readiness, manifestation destruction, Collapse Commitment, severe core distress, the fall into silence, renewed availability. No sensation, alert or interface is defined.

**It belongs to the bound relationship, not to custody.** Picking up a dropped Tuber or Anchor grants no bond, no astral authorization and no Anchor-mediated knowledge (AMO-D065, AMO-Q091). **An empty Anchor transmits nothing living**: if the individual is lost, the surviving object does not report the dead plant's state as though it existed. Whether it keeps any historical or magical residue is not decided (AMO-D132).

**It supports judgement and never solves it.** The Anchor helps the Warden read the individual; it is not an internal scanner computing the right roster decision.

## 11. Warden–Amorpho Astral Attunement

> **Astral Attunement** is the relationship-specific depth to which one Warden can understand one persistent individual through the Anchor.

It is not a species property, not shared by every Warden, not a property of the Anchor object alone, and not general biological knowledge (AMO-D167). Two things reinforce each other without merging: **general Warden biological knowledge** — lifecycle concepts, species tendencies, environments, patterns — and **individual Attunement** — how *this* individual feels through the Anchor, its rhythms, its characteristic transitions, its departures from its own history.

```
BIOLOGICAL TRUTH
      ▼
ASTRAL ANCHOR SIGNAL
      ▼
WARDEN INTERPRETATION  ← shaped by Attunement
```

**It improves interpretation, not biology.** The plant does not change because it is better understood. Attunement may improve resolution, confidence, context and the ability to tell familiar patterns apart. Two compatible explanations are left open: the Warden learns to read the signal better, and the bond itself may come to carry finer feedback. Both may hold.

**It deepens through meaningful shared history, not repetition.** Design space includes long genuine connection, repeated lifecycle observation, several manifestation cycles, repeated embodiment, witnessed damage and recovery, dormancy and reactivation, Bloom, major transitions and long-term care. Rapid entry and exit to farm it is explicitly rejected; no weights, requirements or anti-grind rules are defined.

**Coarse first, finer later.** A new connection gives strong broad cues — presence or absence, severe distress, major instability, availability changes, connection loss — and **no intimate understanding at once**. Long shared history may let the Warden tell apart conditions that once felt alike; illustrative only, never canon for wording or sensation: *this feels like ordinary depletion rather than the harm I have sensed before*; *this Leaf looks terrible, but the individual feels stable*; *the silence is beginning to change in a familiar way*.

**Never telemetry.** Even deep Attunement does not reveal exact reserves, Core Viability, opportunity, Recovery Ceiling, reactivation time, pathology classification or any guaranteed outcome.

**Deep Dormancy remains silent.** Attunement opens no channel through Astral Silence and no control path into the dormant Tuber (AMO-D060, AMO-D088).

**The return of the connection is information.** The signal returns in Pre-Emergence — after Reactivation Commitment has already happened — so its return means *this individual is becoming available to the astral relationship again*. A long-attuned Warden may recognise a familiar early return sooner or more confidently than a new one. It does **not** mean a playable body exists: Reactivation → Pre-Emergence → Emergence → Full Deployment → Astral Readiness remain separate gates (AMO-D160, AMO-D107, AMO-D086). The Anchor communicates; it never causes reactivation.

**It follows the persistent individual.** Leaf → dormancy → next Leaf is one relationship, so Attunement survives manifestation turnover; some manifestation-specific detail is new with each body, and what transfers is not defined.

**It does not transfer with custody**, and it is not an ordinary human progression dimension: it belongs to one Warden–individual pair, adds no combat power, makes no Amorpho stronger and overrides no rule (AMO-D069, L15). Whether a rebinding could ever form a new Attunement, how it might degrade or recover, and whether it becomes a dedicated progression line are **future design**, recorded under AMO-Q131.

> **A long-known Amorpho can be easier to read without becoming easier to calculate.** That is a long-term relationship reward with no stat attached.

## 12. Owner and opponent

**Owner knowledge may exceed opponent knowledge** (AMO-D166). The bound Warden has the individual's history, its care, and Anchor feedback interpreted through Attunement. An opponent ordinarily has the visible body, manifestation type, protection, behaviour and whatever history they independently know — and **no access to the owner's Anchor feedback or Attunement**.

That asymmetry is strategically useful ([37](37_MANIFESTATION_DESTRUCTION_TUBER_CORE_VIABILITY_AND_PHYSICAL_DROP_V0.md) §7, [41](41_BLOOM_EMBODIMENT_CORE_SECURITY_AND_DESTRUCTION_VALUE_V0.md)): it leaves room for risk judgement, uncertainty about pushing toward destruction, and bluff. Behaviour can reveal information without any signalling mechanic — refusing to withdraw, guarding one body heavily, avoiding risk with another, showing a Bloom. No PvP information system or bluff mechanic is designed.

## 13. Roster-decision integration

**Information is valuable because agency is scarce.** One Consciousness means the Warden cannot answer every biological opportunity, so knowing which individual stands where matters (AMO-D028). Collection depth multiplies the judgements without any cooldown information.

**Biological Deployment Cost is a judgement, not a stat.** The Warden knows location, visible condition, known recovery, whether the individual is rooted and working, and its history; they infer remaining opportunity, Core security, recovery potential and reactivation timing (AMO-D161). **Combat Suitability may be known differently** — through equipment, technique, mastery, the body and the objective — and the two axes need not share a certainty.

**The decision stays the Warden's.** The system may surface facts and cues; it never computes *Recommended Amorpho: B*. Decision skill includes managing uncertainty — *I cannot be sure how secure this core is, but I know enough of its history to judge whether this fight is worth it*.

## 14. Worked traces

**A — seasonal A versus extended-growth B.** The Warden knows A's history well enough to judge it is approaching dormancy in a short valuable window, and knows B's strategy offers longer availability. They infer that using A probably costs more opportunity, without knowing how much. The decision remains meaningful.

**B — dormant C.** C is deeply dormant and silent. The Warden knows its prior lifecycle pattern and that conditions are now favourable, and judges it may be nearing readiness — but cannot know when, cannot sense it through silence and cannot force it. When the signal returns, a long-attuned Warden may recognise the familiar pattern early; a body is still several gates away.

**C — two battered fighters.** Two Leaf Amorphos look similarly scarred. The owner knows A had a long productive rooted history and B was embodied early for many fights, and the Anchor may convey that A feels internally steadier. The owner infers A's core is probably more secure. An opponent sees two similar bodies and cannot draw that inference.

**D — Bloom opponent.** An opponent sees a Bloom and infers substantial prior core capability, not current salvageability. Its owner may know more, from history and the Anchor.

**E — fresh injury.** A Leaf is freshly damaged; the Warden sees missing architecture, and ceiling and functional recovery are uncertain. After a rooted recovery period, actual stabilization and returning function become new evidence. **Information evolves through observation.**

**F — wrong but reasonable.** History and feedback suggested a robust core; hidden prior persistent harm made it less secure, and destruction left a marginal survivor. The judgement was reasonable, the outcome differed, and the cause was real biology. **Allowed.**

**G — the forbidden case.** Every channel indicated a stable, well-provisioned core, nothing in the history explains anything else, and the exposed Tuber is inexplicably dead. **Not allowed** — that would be hidden randomness, not biology.

## 16. Deferred presentation and design

AMO-Q044 keeps everything about presentation and precision: player-facing cues, history-recording experience, examination and diagnostics, information precision, accessibility, learning and tutorial, opponent information and any forecasting tool. AMO-Q111 keeps how maturity and core are perceived and shown; AMO-Q119 routing prognosis; AMO-Q115 Signal strength, fidelity and what the Anchor can convey phase by phase, including during embodiment; AMO-Q092 the Radar; AMO-Q091 rebinding and empty Anchors; AMO-Q099 any Warden-wide perception dimension, which remains unaccepted; **AMO-Q131** Attunement. No interface is designed.

**Result:** the Warden reads the plant — through its body, its history, its kind, its surroundings and the quiet channel of the Anchor — and the longer they have known it, the better they read. Nothing reads it for them, and nothing turns it into a number.
