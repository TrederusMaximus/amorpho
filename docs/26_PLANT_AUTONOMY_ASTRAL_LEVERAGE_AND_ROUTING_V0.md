# 26 — Plant Autonomy, Astral Leverage and Biological Routing, v0

**Status:** conceptual specification. It answers **who selects a route** when a biological routing point offers more than one, and what astral power can and cannot do about it. It defines no durations, thresholds, probabilities, weights, autonomous algorithm, AI technology, radar interface, connection mechanic, combat mapping or species behaviour. The direction is owner-supplied game-system input; no botanical research entered (AMO-D024).

It extends [25_TUBER_FUNDED_MANIFESTATION_AND_REPLACEMENT_ROUTING_V0.md](25_TUBER_FUNDED_MANIFESTATION_AND_REPLACEMENT_ROUTING_V0.md), and sits beside [09_EMBODIMENT_AND_ASTRAL_TRANSFER.md](09_EMBODIMENT_AND_ASTRAL_TRANSFER.md), [17_LIFE_CYCLE_STATE_MACHINE_V0.md](17_LIFE_CYCLE_STATE_MACHINE_V0.md) and [20_BIOLOGY_MAGIC_BOUNDARY_AND_ASTRAL_READINESS_V0.md](20_BIOLOGY_MAGIC_BOUNDARY_AND_ASTRAL_READINESS_V0.md).

> Biology decides what is possible. The plant decides what is best for itself. Magic may decide otherwise — and pays the same biological bill.
> Reachable is not playable. Awareness is not agency. Ownership is not access.

## 1. Purpose

The previous pass established that biological eligibility is evaluated before strategy, and that an unattended individual must route autonomously rather than pause the World (AMO-D096, AMO-D097). It deliberately left open **who chooses among eligible routes, and how a Warden could reach that choice at all** (AMO-Q119).

This document supplies the conceptual boundary, in three layers and one named transitional period. It resolves ownership of the decision; it does not design the decision procedure.

## 2. The persistent Tuber, and the rule that it is never played

The **Tuber is the persistent biological individual** (AMO-D087, AMO-D095). Leaf and Bloom are temporary manifestations it funds. Identity, genetics, lineage, Developmental Maturity, condition, reserves, persistent harm and life-cycle history live with the Tuber across active phases, damage, Replacement Emergence, retreat, dormancy and later emergence (AMO-D058, AMO-D080).

That centrality is biological, not playable:

> **The player never moves through the World as a Tuber** (AMO-D089, AMO-D099).

No Tuber locomotion, Tuber combat, Tuber exploration or crawling-tuber camera exists or may be introduced under any of the concepts below. When no playable manifestation exists, the individual may still be alive, biologically active, astrally detectable and astrally reachable — and none of that makes it an avatar.

## 3. Reachable is not inhabitable: the Astral Window

The distinction is already in the architecture as **Astral Signal** versus **Astral Inhabitability** (AMO-D088). This pass gives its transitional case a name.

| | Question | Answer here |
|---|---|---|
| **Astrally reachable** | can the Warden still reach *this individual*? | the persistent Tuber may be, for a transitional biological period |
| **Inhabitable** | is there a developed manifestation that can become the Warden-controlled body? | no, until a manifestation exists and the ordinary gates open |

The **Astral Window** is the transitional biological period during which a persistent Tuber with no viable playable manifestation remains astrally reachable — signal present, access transitional — before Deep Dormancy closes it (AMO-D099). It is what the existing narrowing-access and fading-signal curves already describe at the moment a manifestation ends ([17](17_LIFE_CYCLE_STATE_MACHINE_V0.md) §6, [20](20_BIOLOGY_MAGIC_BOUNDARY_AND_ASTRAL_READINESS_V0.md) §8), not a new mechanism.

Within it:

- the individual may appear to its Warden as reachable;
- direct astral presence with the persistent individual may be established;
- a biological routing decision may still be available, if the plant has not already taken that route.

Reachability alone does not hold a choice open. Autonomous biology may begin a route while the window remains open; direct presence can influence only a routing opportunity that still exists ([28_ASTRAL_WINDOW_END_TO_END_TRACE_V0.md](28_ASTRAL_WINDOW_END_TO_END_TRACE_V0.md)).

**Its duration is biological and state-dependent.** No span, boundary point, warning or display is defined (AMO-Q085, AMO-Q092, AMO-Q115).

### Deep Dormancy closes it

Once the individual enters Deep Dormancy the window is over: Astral Silence, no signal, no entry, no strategic influence, and no revisiting the route already taken (AMO-D060, AMO-D088). The individual remains alive and persistent, and ordinary future-cycle emergence remains possible on its own biological terms. There is no exception, no deep-dormancy override and no retroactive rewrite of a route already executed.

That is the deadline: real, biologically caused, and requiring no countdown interface.

### Presence in the window is not embodiment

A Warden present at a Tuber during the window does not walk, fight, explore or occupy a body. The purpose of presence is **influence over a biologically available routing decision**, and nothing else. Playable embodiment resumes only when a suitable manifestation exists and passes the ordinary gates, including Astral Readiness (AMO-D063, AMO-D086).

Because no manifestation is animated, nothing is being run on magical state, so the suspension of biology that embodiment causes does not apply (AMO-D084): the individual's biology continues throughout the window — which is precisely why the window can close while the Warden is present.

## 4. Autonomous biology optimizes for the individual

When no Warden reaches the routing point, **the plant routes itself** (AMO-D097). What it routes *for* is now settled (AMO-D098):

> Autonomous routing tends toward the individual's own long-term biological viability and future developmental and reproductive potential.

It does not optimize for the player's combat needs, travel plans, desire for another playable body, entertainment value, or the protection of strategic assets at biological cost. It is not a game director, not an NPC personality, not a hidden remote player command, and not a coin flip.

Nor is it omniscient. It works from **currently available biological state, not perfect foresight** — future weather, future damage and future human intervention are not known to it. An autonomous route may therefore turn out badly without the model being wrong.

### It is environment-aware

The decision cannot exist apart from the World. Conceptual inputs — categories only, with no variables, weights or formula (AMO-Q101):

- current Environmental Fit and its ongoing trajectory;
- **Remaining Productive Opportunity**, biological rather than a season timer (AMO-D091);
- current Tuber condition, reserves and developmental context;
- current manifestation state after the damaging event.

### It is not random behaviour

A future model may express tendency as probability, weighted propensity, deterministic utility or a hybrid; that choice is open (AMO-Q101, AMO-Q075). What is closed is the substitute:

> A fixed split such as *half replace, half go dormant*, independent of biological and environmental state, is forbidden.

Randomness may add variability. It may not stand in for biological reasoning. No percentage, table, curve or roll is defined here, and no AI technology — behaviour tree, utility AI, GOAP, learned model — is chosen; *autonomous biology* is a design concept, not an implementation.

## 5. Replacement versus retreat, chosen by the plant

Autonomous route evaluation does **not** begin because the Leaf is damaged, scarred, imperfect or declining. It begins at the functional-collapse routing point defined in [33](33_MATURE_LEAF_FUNCTIONAL_COLLAPSE_BOUNDARY_V0.md) — the point at which the current Leaf can no longer meaningfully serve as the active manifestation and its loss is committed (AMO-D114, AMO-D115, L56). While the Leaf remains serviceable, the biological answer is to keep using it ([24](24_SAME_PHASE_LEAF_RECOVERY_V0.md), AMO-D096).

At that routing point, with replacement biologically eligible (AMO-D096), autonomous biology is weighing two real futures:

| | **Replacement Emergence** | **Retreat toward dormancy** |
|---|---|---|
| Gains | salvages remaining productive opportunity; restores biological capability; continues active development | conserves persistent capacity; avoids wasteful late investment; protects the next cycle |
| Costs | further Tuber expenditure; emergence time; thinner reserves; less buffer against the next adverse event | abandons the opportunity that remained |

The intuitive relationship is preserved without encoding it:

```text
early collapse  + strong Tuber + good conditions + substantial opportunity  → replacement more biologically attractive
late collapse   + weakening conditions + little remaining opportunity       → conservation more biologically attractive
```

**Declining an affordable replacement is a legitimate outcome**, not a failure of the simulation. Abandoning the damaged manifestation, consolidating the Tuber and waiting for a better cycle may be the best available biological strategy (AMO-D092, AMO-Q087).

Autonomy is **plant-protective, not player-punitive**: an unattended individual attempts the best decision biologically available to it. That is a baseline, not a promise of perfect outcomes.

## 6. Three layers of routing

```text
BIOLOGICAL ROUTING POINT
   │
   ├─ Layer 1 — FEASIBILITY          what can this Tuber biologically do?          (biology alone)
   ├─ Layer 2 — AUTONOMOUS PREFERENCE what serves this individual's continuation?   (the plant)
   └─ Layer 3 — ASTRAL OVERRIDE      is direct astral presence established, and
                                      does the Warden select another feasible route? (the player)
   │
   ▼
biology executes the selected route → persistent consequences apply
```

Canonical v0 agency model, replacing the looser *"if multiple routes exist and agency is available, the player may choose"*:

```text
determine feasible biological routes
      ↓
determine the autonomous biological preference
      ↓
is this individual reachable, and is direct astral presence established in time?
      ↓
 no  → the autonomous route executes
 yes → the Warden may select among feasible routes,
        including one autonomous biology would not prefer
      ↓
biology executes the selected route; its costs and consequences are unchanged
```

Layer 1 is never negotiable. Layer 3 never reaches outside Layer 1. Layer 2 is what happens whenever Layer 3 is absent — which is most of the time, for most of a collection.

## 7. Warden leverage, and its limits

Astral power is genuinely large. Within feasibility a Warden may compel another emergence, keep an individual active where autonomous biology would retreat, animate a manifestation and carry the individual across the World, and root it where the plant would never have settled.

It is bounded in two ways.

**Feasibility is hard.** Magical leverage does not create biological capacity from nothing (AMO-D100, L52). A Tuber that cannot fund a replacement will not produce one because the Warden wishes it; there is no infinite re-emergence, no impossible structure, and no survival after death.

**Cost is undiscounted.** A compelled route costs exactly what the same route costs when the plant chooses it: the same Tuber expenditure, the same emergence time, the same reduced manifestation given current biological state, the same thinner buffer afterwards. Magic does not waive the bill — otherwise astral intervention becomes a free optimizer and every earlier cost model quietly collapses.

### Presence must be established, and there is only one of it

Strategic influence requires **direct astral presence** with that individual, through the existing access path — a physical Anchor, an accessible phase and a reachable individual (AMO-D063, AMO-D101). Three things that do **not** grant it:

| Not agency | Why |
|---|---|
| **ownership or custody** | ownership is not availability (L43); an owned plant routes autonomously if no presence is established |
| **radar awareness** | the Radar reports living connections, not control (AMO-D088, AMO-D061) — *reachable* is information, not a command channel |
| **inhabiting another Amorpho nearby** | that changes the troubled plant's World context, not who selects its route |

And presence is **singular**: it occupies the same one consciousness that full embodiment occupies (AMO-D028, AMO-D101). While intervening directly with one individual, the Warden is not simultaneously acting as the human or through another Amorpho. **Astral Capacity** still bounds how many Anchor relationships can be sustained at once; it never becomes simultaneous control (AMO-D067, L44).

So two individuals reaching critical routing periods at the same moment is a genuine dilemma, and the one not attended routes itself. No scheduling, queuing or prioritisation system is designed here (L37).

### Human physical agency is a different power

| | Acts on | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Human physical agency** | the reality around the plant | moving a pot, improving greenhouse conditions, watering, relocating, physical rescue |
| **Astral agency** | a viable internal strategic routing decision | selecting a feasible route the plant would not have selected |

Changing the environment changes the inputs the plant reasons from; it is not an override, and the plant still selects. Both remain available, and neither substitutes for the other (AMO-D062, L42, AMO-Q105).

## 8. Plant interest is not Warden utility

This divergence is intended, and is not sanitised.

A player may keep an individual active when conservation was the better biological course, force a late replacement, spend reserves the plant would have kept, carry an animated Amorpho through hostile climates, or root it somewhere strategically useful and biologically poor. All of this is available, and all of it is paid for biologically.

The consequences arrive through the systems that already exist — condition, reserves, Developmental Maturity, future manifestation potential, and Pathological Tuber Impact where actual persistent loss occurs (AMO-D092, AMO-D093). Repeated aggressive use accumulates there.

Explicitly **not** built: an abuse meter, a morality score, a plant happiness state, a consent dialogue or emotional AI. For v0 this is optimization conflict between a biological individual's self-interest and a player's strategic utility, and biology itself carries the consequence. Whether a later design wants anything more is a separate decision, not a gap to fill quietly.

### The plant does not need the Warden to live

Amorphos are not helpless without a player. They grow, stabilize, retreat, go dormant, emerge and may replace manifestations under their own biology, whether their Warden is embodied elsewhere, travelling as the human, or absent entirely (AMO-D009, L7). The Warden supplies extraordinary agency, never ordinary life.

## 9. Movement and rooting: the same leverage, wider consequences

A rooted plant grows where it is. An inhabited Amorpho can leave, travel, seek another environment and root elsewhere (AMO-D054). The Warden can therefore produce geographic outcomes autonomous biology could never reach — including deliberately worse ones, for strategic reasons.

> Warden mobility decouples plant geography from autonomous biological preference.

And then it stops. Once rooting occurs, magic ends and **Environmental Fit becomes authoritative again** (AMO-D033, AMO-D037, L39): the site's conditions meet this individual's requirements, and the trajectory that follows may be strongly negative, neutral or genuinely good. Magic enables the movement; it does not exempt the plant from where it was put.

This is the general shape of the whole pass:

> Fantasy creates agency; reality sends the bill (L52).

## 10. Autonomy persists while the player is elsewhere

Nothing here introduces a `WAITING_FOR_PLAYER` state, and none may be added. The World never pauses for an owner: the plant continues biologically whether the player is inhabiting another Amorpho, travelling as the human, offline or simply not interested (AMO-D009, AMO-D097).

If a Warden wants to intervene, presence must be established **before the biological opportunity closes**. Missing it is not a punishment; it is the ordinary condition of a persistent world with one consciousness in it.

## 11. Worked scenarios

**A — autonomous replacement.** A primary Leaf fails catastrophically early. The Tuber is strong, the environment favourable, substantial opportunity remains, replacement is feasible. Autonomous preference favours replacement; no Warden intervenes. Replacement Emergence proceeds, a smaller new Leaf develops, the persistent World continues.

**B — autonomous conservation.** A primary Leaf fails late. The Tuber could technically fund a replacement, but conditions are deteriorating and little useful opportunity remains. Autonomous preference favours conservation; no Warden intervenes. The individual retreats through Senescence, capacity preserved for a later cycle (AMO-D070, AMO-Q087).

**C — Warden overrides the preference.** Same state as B; replacement remains feasible but suboptimal for the plant. The Astral Window is open, the Warden establishes direct astral presence, and needs an active Amorpho for a purpose of their own. Replacement is compelled. The Tuber spends capacity, the new manifestation forms over biological time, and the Warden may inhabit it later once it is Astral Ready. Every biological consequence stands. This is the canonical divergence of Warden utility from plant utility.

**D — impossible override.** The Leaf is destroyed; the Tuber is too compromised to fund a replacement. The window is open and the Warden is present. Replacement is not feasible and cannot be compelled; retreat remains the only route. Leverage is not capacity.

**E — movement against autonomous biology.** A healthy Amorpho is rooted somewhere suitable. The Warden inhabits it, travels, and roots it in a strategically useful but biologically poor region. Magic ends; Environmental Fit governs the rooted individual, which then responds autonomously to what was done to it (AMO-D046–AMO-D050).

**F — the window is missed.** A Leaf collapses and the Tuber becomes reachable. The Warden is occupied elsewhere — one consciousness, another individual. No presence is established. The plant routes autonomously, enters Deep Dormancy, and the window closes. The Warden returns later and cannot rewrite the route; the next opportunity is the next biological one.

## 12. The model against the required cases

| Case | How |
|---|---|
| a reachable Tuber is not a playable body | reachability and inhabitability are separate questions (§3, AMO-D099) |
| good conditions, strong Tuber, opportunity left → replacement may be autonomous | autonomous optimization for the individual (§4–§5, AMO-D098) |
| late collapse, poor prospects → dormancy may be autonomous | conservation is a legitimate biological strategy (§5) |
| the Warden may compel a feasible but suboptimal route | Layer 3 selects inside Layer 1 (§6–§7, AMO-D100) |
| an infeasible route cannot be compelled | magic creates no biological capacity (§7, L52) |
| Deep Dormancy ends the opportunity | the window closes; no retroactive override (§3, AMO-D060) |
| no Tuber walking or fighting | presence is influence, not embodiment (§2–§3, AMO-D089) |
| no simultaneous intervention in two individuals | presence is singular (§7, AMO-D028, AMO-D101) |
| a badly chosen rooting site still costs | Environmental Fit resumes authority (§9) |
| a compelled replacement is not cheaper | no magical discount (§7, AMO-D100) |
| an unattended plant is not frozen or punished | autonomy is plant-protective and always runs (§5, §10) |
