# 22 — Biological Year Walkthrough, v0

**Status:** canonical, nonnumeric integration walkthrough. One abstract individual makes one circuit from Deep Dormancy through Active Leaf and back. “Year” names that life-cycle circuit, not a calendar duration or a claim about any real species. This tests whether the existing models compose; it defines no new biological rule, mechanism, value or threshold.

The trace uses [Environmental Fit v0](12_ENVIRONMENT_AND_FIT_MODEL_V0.md), [condition v0](14_CURRENT_BIOLOGICAL_CONDITION_V0.md), the [life-cycle state machine](17_LIFE_CYCLE_STATE_MACHINE_V0.md), [Developmental Maturity](19_DEVELOPMENTAL_MATURITY_V0.md), the [biology ↔ magic boundary](20_BIOLOGY_MAGIC_BOUNDARY_AND_ASTRAL_READINESS_V0.md) and [Pathological Tuber Impact](21_PATHOLOGICAL_TUBER_IMPACT_V0.md). Its counterfactuals test the same architecture without adding extra years to the main trace.

## The individual at the start

**Abstract Individual A** is a living, rooted Tuber at one unspecified location on Earth. It is in **Deep Dormancy**. Its identity, genotype, provenance, condition, reserves and Developmental Maturity persist from earlier life; none is recreated when a new phase begins. For this trace, condition is sound, stress is low, and reserves are adequate. These are qualitative starting assumptions, not species facts.

No Leaf or Bloom is active. A physical Astral Anchor remains attached and the Warden has capacity for this connection, but Deep Dormancy closes astral entry and silences the **Astral Signal**. The Anchor supplies neither a signal nor a location fix merely by existing. A successful Deep Dormancy period restores **Astral Readiness** before the next active availability window; that magical recovery does not restore biological vitality or maturity. The Tuber remains biologically simulated in its local environment throughout dormancy, even with no playable manifestation (AMO-D084, AMO-D086, AMO-D088, AMO-D093).

## Emergence into Leaf

Individual A passes through **Pre-Emergence**, then **Emergence**, then **Active Leaf**. This is one permitted route through the seven-state topology, not a universal species sequence. The life-cycle system owns the transitions; their triggers and timing remain open (AMO-Q084, AMO-Q101).

The signal can return faintly in Pre-Emergence before entry is possible. Access widens through Emergence; its exact opening point is open (AMO-Q085). Once Active Leaf is established, the phase permits entry, subject separately to the Anchor relationship and biological inhabitability. A new Leaf is a temporary manifestation of **the same individual**. Persistent Tuber condition, reserves, maturity, identity and genetics did not reset or split. The Leaf now offers biological productive opportunity; how that opportunity is represented is AMO-Q116.

## Rooted, healthy work

The World supplies changing **Local Environment State** at A's Earth location. In this part of the trace its conditions are favourable for A. The World owns the conditions; it does not label them favourable. **Environmental Fit** evaluates those conditions against A's effective biological response profile. Its condition direction can be stable while its growth/recovery opportunity is strong: the two outputs must be read together (AMO-D035–AMO-D037, AMO-D049).

Rooted in Active Leaf, A performs useful biological work. Stress stays low or relieves, reserves can rebuild, and the Tuber gains persistent development. In this illustrative year, enough actual productive growth occurs that **Developmental Maturity advances**. Fit supplied opportunity; the biological individual converted work and capacity into persistent development. Fit did not award maturity directly (AMO-D081). Good conditions therefore do more than keep A alive: they support accumulation after recovery is complete. **Surviving is not thriving** (L38).

Leaf structure and persistent Tuber state remain distinct. A healthy visible Leaf is useful here, but appearance alone would not prove how much work it performed.

### Brief Warden interlude

At this active, biologically inhabitable point, assume A's Anchor connection is valid and the Warden enters. The biological Leaf supplies the form and its starting condition; the magical layer runs the inhabited fighter. While inhabited, A's normal biology pauses: no Leaf productivity, maturity growth, phase progress, biological recovery or Programmed Tuber Draw occurs. The rest of the World continues (AMO-D084). Productive opportunity may pass while World conditions change; embodiment does not itself spend Tuber reserves or cause pathology.

Assume magical exertion lowers **Astral Readiness**. When the Warden exits, A returns to a **rooted Active Leaf**, not to dormancy; neither its Leaf structure nor its biological state is reset. Readiness also does not reset on exit. Rooted biological life can restore it under suitable Fit, at an unspecified pace. A can be biologically healthy but astrally tired, or biologically compromised but astrally ready; readiness is not a health proxy (AMO-D072, AMO-D086). Alive, phase-accessible and biologically inhabitable remain separate tests for entry (AMO-D031, AMO-D063).

## One disturbance, followed by recovery

Later, one abstract local disturbance visibly damages the Leaf and temporarily reduces its function. Its concrete cause and the event-to-structure rule are deliberately absent (AMO-Q020, AMO-Q086). The **Leaf Functional Capacity** that remains is still substantial. A experiences some stress, uses some reserves and misses productive opportunity. The Leaf stays visibly impaired for this phase; favourable Fit does not magically repair its structure (AMO-Q103).

The disturbance passes. The World again supplies favourable local conditions, Fit offers strong positive opportunity, and much **Remaining Productive Opportunity** remains in this active phase. A has adequate condition and reserves and still performs useful work. In this branch that later work is sufficient for the Tuber to remain sound and for some persistent development to accrue. The year may be less productive than it could have been, but **no Pathological Tuber Impact occurs** (AMO-D091, AMO-D092, L51).

What was lost has several horizons. The current Leaf lost structural integrity and some function; stress rose temporarily; reserves were used and some productive opportunity passed. None of those observations alone says that Tuber vitality fell or maturity regressed. The missed *possible* gain is not actual persistent loss. The trace does not assume the Leaf repaired itself, nor does it require a catch-up bonus (AMO-Q116).

## Counterfactual: modest appearance, real persistent loss

Rewind to approximately the same Active Leaf phase and change the circumstances, not the individual's identity. The Leaf looks only moderately impaired, but its biological function is strongly compromised. Fit stays poor, condition and reserves are less favourable, and little productive opportunity remains. Earlier lost time cannot be recreated by a late improvement. Insufficient work persists, and in this counterfactual **actual accumulated Tuber development regresses**: indirect, cumulative Pathological Tuber Impact has occurred (AMO-D090–AMO-D092).

The listed inputs make loss *possible*, not automatic. AMO-Q116 must explain productive integration and AMO-Q118 must determine where this case crosses from no gain into actual loss. No percentage of visible Leaf damage decides it. A retreat into Senescence might prevent further loss, but cannot undo loss already incurred; the retreat trigger remains AMO-Q087 and AMO-Q101. The damaged Leaf will end, while maturity regression persists into dormancy and needs future biological growth to rebuild. A different pathological outcome could reduce vitality instead or as well; that does not follow in a fixed proportion. An alive individual with sufficiently compromised condition may be uninhabitable, but the entry rule remains AMO-Q042.

This branch illustrates **cumulative** impact without a catastrophic instant. An **acute** event could also cause rapid persistent loss under the accepted model; no event rule or threshold is supplied here. The two harm pathways, direct and indirect, can each be acute or cumulative (AMO-D090).

## Side checks the Leaf year must not erase

**Bloom spending.** If A were eligible and took a different permitted route from Emergence into **Bloom**, the Bloom would be a sibling active state, not a required stage after Leaf. Flowering maturity enables this route but does not trigger or guarantee it (AMO-D071, AMO-D078; AMO-Q084, AMO-Q107). A rooted Bloom may intentionally draw stored Tuber capacity through **Programmed Tuber Draw**. The immediate stored state can fall without injury, maturity regression or Pathological Tuber Impact. A future productive Leaf phase may rebuild what was spent. A net resource decline therefore cannot diagnose pathology; the rejected “Tuber Balance” shortcut remains rejected (AMO-D087, AMO-D092). No reproduction occurs in this check, so the Evolutionator has no handoff to perform.

**Dormant direct harm.** In a separate counterfactual, A has no Leaf while dormant. If a future-defined harmful exposure actually compromises its persistent Tuber integrity, that is **direct Pathological Tuber Impact** without any productivity or manifestation-damage path (AMO-D090). The same conceptual possibility applies if a Tuber is excavated: unrooted is neither automatically harmed nor frozen in time (AMO-D093). Which conditions and durations cause harm belong to AMO-Q117; this walkthrough names none.

## Retreat and the end state

For the main, recoverable-damage trace, the Active Leaf eventually enters **Senescence**, then **Early Dormancy**, then **Deep Dormancy**. This uses the ordinary route without inventing a trigger or duration. The Leaf and its visible damage end with the manifestation. Productive work and condition changes have already affected the persistent individual over time; dormancy is not a special moment that awards maturity or erases harm (AMO-D070, AMO-D083).

The Astral Signal fades through Senescence, may linger faintly in Early Dormancy and is absent in Deep Dormancy. Access closes through the transition; its exact boundary is open. A successful Deep Dormancy period restores Astral Readiness before the next active window, **without healing any pathological Tuber loss** (AMO-D086, AMO-D088).

| Persistent aspect | Start of this trace | End of main trace | End of loss counterfactual |
|---|---|---|---|
| Identity and genotype | Individual A | same individual | same individual |
| Life-cycle state and manifestation | Deep Dormancy; no Leaf | Deep Dormancy; damaged Leaf gone | Deep Dormancy; impaired Leaf gone |
| Vitality | sound | sound; no pathological loss | may remain sound if loss chiefly affects maturity |
| Stress and reserves | low stress; adequate reserves | temporary burden relieved; reserves remain adequate after work and use | burden and reserves may be less favourable |
| Developmental Maturity | accumulated starting development | advanced through actual productive growth, even if less than otherwise possible | regressed through actual persistent loss |
| Astral state | signal silent; entry closed | signal silent; entry closed; readiness restored by successful Deep Dormancy before the next window | signal and readiness follow their own rules, not the maturity setback |

These are distinct state dimensions, not a combined score. “Approximately unchanged” is also a possible persistent biological outcome for a less productive year; failure to gain is not regression. In the loss branch, no phase change clears actual Tuber injury. In neither branch does disappearing Leaf damage itself become inherited by the next Leaf (AMO-D058, AMO-D082, AMO-D092).

## Ownership and unresolved handoffs

| Handoff in the trace | Established owner | What remains open |
|---|---|---|
| Earth location → local conditions; a separate abstract disturbance | **World** owns local environmental truth; a discrete event has no v0 representation | event representation and effects (AMO-Q020), local resolution and time step (AMO-Q005, AMO-Q074) |
| Local conditions × A's response profile → biological trajectory | **Environmental Fit** evaluates; stores no parallel environment or biology | aggregation and exposure history (AMO-Q070–AMO-Q072) |
| Trajectory and Leaf function over remaining opportunity → Tuber outcome | **Biological individual / Amorpho** owns condition, reserves and development | productivity integration (AMO-Q116), condition/recovery dynamics (AMO-Q073, AMO-Q102), actual-loss crossing (AMO-Q118) |
| Disturbance → Leaf structural and functional impairment | **World** supplies an environmental occurrence; **Amorpho** carries its manifestation consequence | event representation (AMO-Q020), Leaf response and structural-to-functional mapping (AMO-Q086), in-phase repair (AMO-Q103) |
| Persistent biological state → phase transition | **Life-cycle system** owns the transition; Fit only informs it | routing and triggers (AMO-Q084, AMO-Q101), premature retreat (AMO-Q087) |
| Dormant environment → possible direct Tuber harm | **World** supplies conditions; **Amorpho** bears any harm | direct susceptibility, storage and transport (AMO-Q117) |
| Phase, Anchor and condition → entry; embodiment → exit | **Magic / Warden** owns access and animation, with biological gates supplied by the individual | inhabitability and transitional access (AMO-Q042, AMO-Q085), readiness dynamics (AMO-Q112) |
| Reproduction → inheritance | **Evolutionator**, only if reproduction occurs | no handoff in this trace (AMO-Q079) |

The trace closes **conceptually**: each stage has an owner and an allowed successor, and the healthy, recovered and harmed outcomes can coexist without contradiction. The gaps are rules *inside* existing handoffs, not a missing fourth simulation domain. This document does not decide their rates, thresholds, event mechanics, Bloom routing, species interpretation or representation; no new decision, law or open question is required.

The event-to-Leaf handoff is traced separately in [23_ACUTE_EVENT_TO_LEAF_IMPAIRMENT_V0.md](23_ACUTE_EVENT_TO_LEAF_IMPAIRMENT_V0.md), stopping at post-event manifestation state before this year's later productivity or Tuber outcome is considered.
