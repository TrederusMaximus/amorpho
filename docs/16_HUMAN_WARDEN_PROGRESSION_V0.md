# 16 — Human / Warden Progression and Astral Capacity, v0

**Status:** conceptual, version 0. This document establishes that the human character has a progression domain of its own, and defines its first confirmed dimension. It designs no progression system, no curve, no numbers, no requirements, no skill tree and no interface. Where something is undecided it points to [06_OPEN_QUESTIONS.md](06_OPEN_QUESTIONS.md).

> Amorphos create the living world's rapid change. The human accumulates slow, difficult, persistent magical capability.
> Astral Anchors are physical resources. Astral Capacity is human capability.

## 1. Two progression domains

Amorpho has two kinds of long-term advancement, and they are separate (AMO-D066):

| | **Amorpho / World progression** | **Human / Warden progression** |
|---|---|---|
| What advances | growth, development, life-cycle phases, cultivation, reproduction, selection, lineages, generations | magical and astral capability |
| Pace | dynamic — plants grow, phases turn, offspring appear, individuals move, lineages develop | slow, difficult, persistent |
| Carrier | individual plants and populations, which come and go | the human character, which does not |

The player-facing term is not fixed. *Warden Progression*, *Astral Development*, *Warden Capacity* and *Astral Capacity* are all working concepts, and nothing here commits to any of them (AMO-Q100).

Nor is this assumed to be conventional levelling. Levels, ranks, stages, milestones or some other representation entirely are all still possible; no numeric RPG level system is implied (AMO-Q096).

## 2. Why the human progresses slowly

> Human magical advancement should be substantially slower and harder than ordinary change in the Amorpho ecosystem.

The living world is meant to feel busy: things grow, flower, reproduce, decline and move on a biological rhythm the player does not control. Set against that, human development should feel long-term, significant, difficult to accelerate, and genuinely meaningful when it happens.

No time requirement is defined, and nothing yet ties advancement to elapsed world or real time (§9, AMO-Q097).

### The human is the continuity character

Individual Amorphos grow, reproduce, enter dormancy, die, produce offspring, form lineages, get traded and get released. The human persists through all of it.

That makes the human character plausibly the **slowest and most persistent carrier of progression in a player's whole history** — someone may lose or replace many plants over years while their own magical development remains. This is intentional, and it is why casually destroying that progression is architecturally discouraged (§11, AMO-Q098).

## 3. Astral Capacity

The first confirmed dimension of human progression (AMO-D067):

> **Astral Capacity** is the human character's ability to sustain multiple active astral connections at once.

It is deliberately narrow. It means capacity, and nothing else.

In particular, a sustained *connection* is not a presence. Capacity bounds how many active Anchor relationships a Warden can hold open at once; it never lets them act through two individuals at the same moment, because **direct astral presence** — full embodiment, or strategic presence at a reachable individual's routing point — occupies the one consciousness (AMO-D028, AMO-D101, [26_PLANT_AUTONOMY_ASTRAL_LEVERAGE_AND_ROUTING_V0.md](26_PLANT_AUTONOMY_ASTRAL_LEVERAGE_AND_ROUTING_V0.md)).

### Physical Anchors and capacity are different constraints

| | Belongs to | Question |
|---|---|---|
| **Physical Anchor supply** | the world — reusable objects the human possesses (AMO-D061) | *Do I physically have enough Anchors?* |
| **Astral Capacity** | the human — magical capability | *Can I sustain this many active connections?* |

A player may possess more physical Anchors than their capacity lets them sustain simultaneously. Acquiring another Anchor object does not, by itself, widen what the human can hold open.

These are two independent scarcity systems, and which one usually binds is not decided (AMO-Q090, AMO-Q095).

### Where capacity sits relative to the three gates

Capacity does **not** add a fourth gate to astral entry. It sits *upstream* of the first one, bounding how many active Anchor relationships can exist at once (AMO-D063):

```
physical Anchor supply  ∩  Astral Capacity
                 ▼
     active Anchor relationships
                 ▼
   ┌──────── the three gates ────────┐
   │  Anchor · life-cycle · condition │
   └──────────────┬──────────────────┘
                  ▼
       currently playable roster
```

Several independent systems therefore constrain how many individuals a player can actually play, and none of them is the size of the collection.

## 4. Each additional connection is harder

> Increasing simultaneous Astral Capacity becomes progressively more difficult (AMO-D068).

No curve is defined, and specifically **no linear rule** of the form `level N = N Anchors`. Conceptually:

```
first capacity      →  foundational awakening
further capacity    →  increasingly difficult advancement
high capacity       →  exceptional long-term human development
```

### It must not feel like an inventory slot

Gaining one more point of capacity should read as a **deep magical development of the character**, especially at higher capacity — not as `+1 slot`. The difficulty curve and whatever presentation eventually exists should both reinforce that. No ceremony, ritual or interface is designed (AMO-Q095, AMO-Q100).

Very high capacity may reasonably be uncommon and represent substantial player history. Rarity, any maximum, prestige and competitive implications are all undecided (AMO-Q095).

## 5. Capability, not raw power

Human progression primarily expands **what the human can do**, not how hard their Amorphos hit.

A more advanced Warden is **not** assumed to make every Amorpho stronger, faster or more damaging. Whether human development touches combat at all is open, and must respect the law that skill remains central to combat outcomes (L15, AMO-Q025).

Astral Capacity is the **only confirmed dimension**. Other possibilities — astral perception, transfer stability, richer Anchor information, ritual capability, sensitivity to life-cycle transitions, other astral skills — are recorded as possibilities only. None is accepted, and no skill tree exists (AMO-Q099).

## 6. Progression should resist grind

> Human magical advancement should resist trivial grind acceleration (AMO-D066).

It should not collapse into *repeat an activity → farm experience → unlock many Anchors quickly*. The player's magical development is meant to feel more fundamental than that.

What actually advances a Warden is **not decided**: long-term experience, meaningful astral use, successful relationships with individual Amorphos, significant events, mastery, difficult milestones, story progression and other mechanisms are all candidates (AMO-Q096).

## 7. What capacity does not do

Human progression strengthens the human **without invalidating anything else** (AMO-D069).

### It does not override biology

A highly developed Warden still cannot enter an individual that is deeply dormant, one that is too biologically compromised for entry, or one that is dead (AMO-D060, AMO-D031, AMO-D050). Capacity is about how many connections can be held, never about forcing one open.

### It does not override physical reality

Capacity does not move Anchors, locate plants, convert ownership into access, resolve custody, or remove geographic strategy. In particular it leaves the human-hands rule completely intact (L42, AMO-D062):

> The soul may travel; the Anchor must move by human hands.

Even the most advanced Warden reassigns an Anchor by physically travelling to both individuals. Progression expands astral capability; it does not dissolve world constraints.

### It does not limit ownership or cultivation

Capacity limits **simultaneous astral connection**, not how many plants a player may own, cultivate, trade, breed, maintain or establish (AMO-D065). A collector with a large greenhouse and low capacity is a perfectly coherent player.

### It does not touch wild life or offspring

Wild populations and new offspring remain unbound by default and are entirely unaffected by the player's capacity (AMO-D064). A large and successful breeding project neither consumes capacity nor creates it — the player consciously chooses which individuals enter the astral roster, and the rest simply live.

### It does not widen the Astral Radar

Greater capacity may mean **more simultaneous connections shown**, because there are more of them. It is not assumed to improve range, precision, positional information or biological sensing; those would be separate future concepts if they exist at all (AMO-D061, AMO-Q092, AMO-Q099).

## 8. Collection size is not power

> A large collection creates possibilities. It does not create unlimited simultaneous access.

Owning many cultivated plants, wild-collected individuals, offspring and established populations does not convert into a large playable roster. Only a subset can be anchored within capacity, and of those only some are in an accessible phase and well enough to enter (AMO-D063, L43). And even then, **one body at a time** (AMO-D028).

This is deliberate. It keeps a collection a source of options, logistics and choices rather than accumulating directly into power.

## 9. Time is not decided

Amorpho already has a persistent Earth and a time-dependent world (AMO-D045, AMO-D046). Whether human advancement should be constrained by **world time or real elapsed time**, rather than by gameplay activity alone, is deliberately left open in both directions — neither required nor forbidden (AMO-Q097).

It is a question to settle with player experience in mind, not by architecture.

## 10. Two speeds, on purpose

```
AMORPHO / WORLD     ████████████████████  dynamic: cycles, growth, offspring, lineages
HUMAN / WARDEN      ███                   slow: magical capability accumulating over a long history
```

These coexist deliberately, and the contrast may become a defining part of Amorpho's pacing: a world that keeps moving on its own around a character who changes rarely but permanently.

## 11. Loss is not designed

Human death mechanics are not decided, and neither is whether Astral Capacity can ever be lost or regress.

The default architectural direction is to **avoid casually destroying long-term human progression**, given that it is meant to be the slowest thing a player builds. If loss or regression is ever introduced it requires its own explicit decision, not a side effect of some other system (AMO-Q098, AMO-Q026).
