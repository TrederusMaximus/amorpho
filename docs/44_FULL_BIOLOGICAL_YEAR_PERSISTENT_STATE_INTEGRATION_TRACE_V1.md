# 44 — Full Biological Year to Persistent-State Integration Trace, v1

**Status:** integration and consistency trace of accepted rules, not a new design. It follows one abstract persistent individual through a complete cycle and asks what state carries into the next one. It defines no value, rate, formula, duration, calendar, species fact, threshold or mechanism, and it introduces no new subsystem.

**Acceptance question:** do the systems specified *after* the [Biological Year Walkthrough](22_BIOLOGICAL_YEAR_WALKTHROUGH_V0.md) — Productive Return ([39](39_ACTIVE_LEAF_PRODUCTIVE_RETURN_AND_PERSISTENT_TUBER_BENEFIT_V0.md)), Recovery Ceiling ([40](40_ROOTED_MATURE_LEAF_RECOVERY_CEILING_AND_COMPENSATORY_REMODELING_V0.md)), two clocks and lifecycle sovereignty ([42](42_WORLD_TIME_SEASONAL_OPPORTUNITY_AND_EMBODIMENT_V0.md)), the depletion/harm boundary ([43](43_ORDINARY_DEPLETION_TO_PERSISTENT_HARM_BOUNDARY_V0.md)), core viability ([37](37_MANIFESTATION_DESTRUCTION_TUBER_CORE_VIABILITY_AND_PHYSICAL_DROP_V0.md)) and combat body continuity ([35](35_COMBAT_PROTECTION_MANIFESTATION_BODY_DAMAGE_AND_BIOLOGICAL_PERSISTENCE_V0.md)) — compose across one whole cycle without contradiction?

> **The persistent individual survives the year; manifestations do not. The Tuber pays for a Leaf, the Leaf works for the Tuber, and the next Leaf is built from what the Tuber actually became.**

## 1. Relationship to doc 22

[22](22_BIOLOGICAL_YEAR_WALKTHROUGH_V0.md) remains the **foundational** walkthrough and is not superseded: it established that the World, Fit, condition, life-cycle, maturity, magic and pathology models compose across a cycle, and its ownership and handoff tables still hold. This document is the **v1 integration trace**, exercising the systems that did not exist when it was written and answering one question doc 22 could not yet ask: *what persists into the next cycle, and what does not?*

**"Year" means one conceptual full lifecycle cycle for this test individual, not a fixed duration and not a universal rule for every species** (AMO-D147). No calendar is implied.

## 2. The test individual

**Abstract Individual A.** No species, no values. It begins as a **viable dormant Tuber** carrying identity, genetics, lineage, Developmental Maturity, Current Biological Condition, persistent capacity, its history and an attached **Astral Anchor** — reasonably provisioned but not idealized, and **with no Pathological Tuber Impact** (AMO-D058, AMO-D061, AMO-D148). Its lifecycle strategy includes a meaningful active phase followed by a dormancy transition; that is one abstract strategy, not a claim about any real species (AMO-D024, AMO-D147).

No playable manifestation exists yet, and the Tuber is never itself a playable form (AMO-D089, AMO-D099).

## 3. The main trace

**1 — Construction.** A commits to a primary Leaf. Tuber-funded construction accumulates through Emergence to **Full Deployment**, where accounting hands off to the mature manifestation (AMO-D106, AMO-D109, AMO-D111). Full Deployment is not Astral Readiness (AMO-D107).

**2 — Post-construction depletion.** Immediately afterwards A's persistent core is qualitatively **more depleted than before construction**. This is ordinary expenditure, not pathology (AMO-D148). So a freshly successful Leaf coexists with a recently depleted Tuber, and Core Viability is at its weakest point of the cycle: if this manifestation were destroyed now, little might survive (AMO-D131, AMO-D136).

**3 — Rooted productive work.** A stays rooted under favourable conditions with biology running. Functional Capacity, Environmental Fit, current condition and Remaining Productive Opportunity together produce **Active Leaf Work**, and from it **Persistent Productive Return** (AMO-D135, AMO-D081).

**4 — Replenishment before gain.** Early return **rebuilds what construction spent** rather than immediately exceeding A's prior position. Replenishment and net gain stay distinct, and A may be genuinely improving while still below where it started (AMO-D138, AMO-D148).

**5 — Core Viability improves.** Through this rooted period A crosses qualitatively from *little would survive losing the body* toward an **independently viable core** — before the season is anywhere near "successful", and with no threshold defined (AMO-D131, AMO-D151).

**6 — Embodiment.** The Warden inhabits the Leaf. It is the **same physical manifestation**; the Tuber travels inside as the core with its Anchor; ordinary biological progression pauses, including recovery and Productive Return (AMO-D120, AMO-D129, AMO-D132, AMO-D084, AMO-D136).

**7 — The World does not pause.** While A is inhabited the season advances and local rooted opportunity begins to decline. A's own biological state holds where it is, apart from explicit injury (AMO-D145, AMO-D123, L64).

**8 — Missed opportunity.** The embodiment lasts long enough that useful local opportunity passes unused. **No Productive Return occurred in that period, none is banked, and none of it is pathology**; elapsed world time produced no maturity, no return and no stronger core (AMO-D145, AMO-D148).

**9 — Exit while opportunity remains.** The Warden exits before the window closes entirely, so the year continues. A roots at its actual location and **biology resumes from actual state** — no reset, and no catch-up for what was missed (AMO-D032, AMO-D137).

**10 — Mature Leaf damage.** An abstract environmental or mechanical event damages the rooted Leaf: Structural Integrity falls, current Functional Capacity falls, some surviving architecture is permanently lost so the **Recovery Ceiling is reduced**, and the Leaf remains **above Functional Collapse** (AMO-D091, AMO-D141, AMO-D114). The damage belongs to the manifestation; **no automatic Tuber debit follows** (AMO-D111, L55). No cause, pest or pathogen is named (AMO-D024).

**11 — Rehabilitation.** Under rooted conditions: **Structural Stabilization**, **Scarring**, **Compensatory Remodeling**, then substantial **Functional Recovery**. Lost mature architecture does not regrow (AMO-D139, AMO-D094).

**12 — Scarred, stable, functional.** A's Leaf ends up visibly scarred, structurally below pristine, mechanically stable, **significantly functionally recovered**, and permanently capped below its original potential. Appearance, current function and ceiling are three different things (AMO-D140, AMO-D134, L51).

**13 — Rehabilitation costs opportunity, not history.** While A rehabilitated, World time continued and remaining opportunity kept declining. None of it is retroactively restored (AMO-D145).

**14 — Renewed productive work.** With function recovered far enough, the Leaf again produces useful Productive Return — from the opportunity that is **still ahead**, never from what passed (AMO-D137, L61).

**15 — Normal lifecycle retirement.** A's intrinsic lifecycle moves toward its dormancy transition and the Leaf enters **ordinary Senescence**. This is programmed retirement, **not** Functional Collapse and not Collapse Commitment ([17](17_LIFE_CYCLE_STATE_MACHINE_V0.md) §8, AMO-D114). Local conditions may still be reasonably suitable — **good environment is not automatic continued growth**, and no relocation would reset the transition (AMO-D146, L65).

**16 — The Leaf ends.** Its architecture, scars, Structural Integrity, current Functional Capacity and Recovery Ceiling all end with it (AMO-D074, AMO-D141). What the Leaf *did* does not end.

**17 — Dormancy.** A enters Early then Deep Dormancy: non-playable, astrally silent, with a successful dormant period restoring **Astral Readiness only** (AMO-D060, AMO-D086, AMO-D088). Dormancy **awards nothing, refills nothing and erases nothing** — it cannot manufacture return that never occurred, reset condition or clear harm (AMO-D083, AMO-D145, AMO-D149). Entering dormancy in good, moderate or depleted condition is all ordinary; A enters reasonably well provisioned but not perfect.

**18 — Next Emergence.** When A is biologically ready again, the next manifestation is funded **from the persistent state actually carried through dormancy** — never from a generic species baseline (AMO-D095, AMO-D109).

**19 — A new Leaf.** It is **fresh architecture**: no inherited scars, no inherited missing structure, no inherited ceiling (AMO-D074, AMO-D113). Its achievable scale and quality may nonetheless be shaped by the Tuber that funded it, so the next manifestation may be more substantial, similarly supported or less so — derived from persistent biology, never from old Leaf geometry, and never as combat progression (AMO-D095, AMO-Q110, L5).

**Outcome.** A had an expensive, interrupted, damaged year and ends it **viable and meaningfully provisioned** — not maximal. Construction was paid, early return replenished it, embodiment cost real opportunity, damage cost structure and ceiling, rehabilitation restored substantial function, later return added further benefit. **No Pathological Tuber Impact occurred anywhere in this trace** (AMO-D148, AMO-D149).

## 4. What persists, and what does not

| Manifestation-owned — ends with the Leaf | Persistent — carried by the Tuber |
|---|---|
| current Leaf architecture | identity, genetics, lineage |
| scars and visible history of that Leaf | Developmental Maturity |
| Structural Integrity | Current Biological Condition |
| current Functional Capacity | available persistent capacity |
| Recovery Ceiling | state bearing on Core Viability |
| manifestation-specific damage and its recovery trajectory | Pathological Tuber Impact, if any |
| | the Astral Anchor |
| | accumulated persistent history, including realized Productive Return |

These are distinct dimensions, never one score (AMO-D150, AMO-D056). A heavily scarred Leaf may retire and transfer **no** physical mark to its successor, while everything its life produced remains.

**Productive Return is the bridge**: temporary Leaf → useful rooted work → persistent Tuber changes → Leaf disappears → those changes remain → next Leaf built from the changed Tuber. That is the year-over-year persistence engine, and it needs no experience system (L47, L61).

**Rehabilitation protects that bridge** rather than the Leaf: stabilizing and compensating a damaged manifestation restores future function and therefore future return opportunity — it never preserves the Leaf beyond its life (AMO-D139, AMO-D141).

Had pathology existed, it would have crossed every one of these boundaries: Leaf end, dormancy and next Emergence, with fresh architecture built around a still-harmed individual, absent genuine persistent repair (AMO-D149).

## 5. Contrast branches

**A — too much embodiment.** The Leaf deploys, the Warden inhabits early and stays embodied through most of the suitable season. Little Productive Return occurs and world opportunity passes. Rooting comes late; the Leaf may recover biologically, but little productive window remains. A enters dormancy depleted, **with no pathology necessarily anywhere**, and the next cycle begins from a weaker persistent position (AMO-D145, AMO-D148).

**B — a strong season.** Mostly rooted, excellent Fit, strong function, sustained return, no significant damage. A finishes its active phase strongly provisioned, enters dormancy, and its next manifestation has strong biological support potential. No formula produced that.

**C — pathology alongside a productive year.** A productive season occurs **and** a separate direct Tuber injury harms persistent structure or capability. The Leaf still contributes real return; the persistent harm remains; dormancy does not erase it; the next Leaf is fresh architecture around a harmed individual. **Return and pathology are orthogonal** (AMO-D090, AMO-D138, AMO-D149).

**D — a replacement year.** The primary Leaf fails; A funds a Replacement; it deploys and active return resumes. The final persistent outcome reflects **both** the extra construction cost and the later productive work, with no break-even rule and no pathology implied by expense alone (AMO-D096, AMO-D148).

## 7. Finding

> **Yes — one individual can have a difficult but biologically successful year with no reset, no hidden experience system and no pathology shortcut.**

The main trace contains expenditure, missed opportunity, real damage, permanent scarring and a permanently lowered ceiling, and still ends with a viable, meaningfully provisioned Tuber carrying genuine Productive Return into a fresh manifestation. **No new decision, law or question was required**, and no contradiction was found in the audits.

Three properties made that work, and they are worth naming because they are what a later quantitative model must not break:

1. **Temporary and persistent state are cleanly split**, so a ruined Leaf is not a ruined individual and a fresh Leaf is not a fresh individual.
2. **Cost, loss and harm are three different things** — spending, missing and being damaged each leave a different kind of trace.
3. **Nothing in the cycle is a reset**: neither rooting, nor rehabilitation, nor retirement, nor dormancy, nor a new Emergence restores anything the individual did not biologically earn.

What the trace cannot yet say is *how much* — of return, of recovery, of remaining opportunity, or of the crossing into harm. That is the same frontier every pass has left standing, and it is now the only frontier left in this chain.
